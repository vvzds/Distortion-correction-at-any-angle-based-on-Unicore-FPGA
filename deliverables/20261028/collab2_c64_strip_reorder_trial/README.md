# C64 strip reorder prototype (2026-10-28)

Status: isolated RTL prototype, functionally verified and routed as a standalone module. It is **not integrated** with the C63 mapper/cache, DDR path, or full video top. C50 seed7 remains the board-tested fallback; C63 is the routed timing-closed small-angle candidate.

`strip_reorder.v` accepts one RGB565 frame in 16-line output strips, column-major within each strip, and emits raster-ordered pixels through two alternating synchronous RAM banks. It supports valid/ready backpressure in both directions, emits x/y and frame flags, and handles the final eight-line strip of 1080p. Addresses use an increment by 1920 and a column increment instead of a variable multiply. Two 16-line banks hold 122,880 bytes total. The RTL SHA256 is `459159B14CE49C7A04583C608489A5CFD0C6F52AEB8D0221F2B87482B99E3FCF`.

ModelSim passed two consecutive frames with output backpressure at 7x5x2, 8x6x3, 1920x33x16, and full 1920x1080x16 geometries. The separate uninterrupted-input/output test emitted all 2,073,600 pixels in 2,104,388 cycles (1.014848 cycles/pixel), equivalent to a **59.40 frames/s reorder-only** limit at 125 MHz. It does not include mapping, source-cache misses, DDR arbitration, or display timing. Testbenches and raw logs are in `evidence/simulation/`; run `vsim -batch -do "do run_sim.do"` from an ASCII-only copy of this directory.

The standalone PG2L100H-FBG676 seed7 PDS build mapped to 32/155 DRM blocks and 223/66,600 LUTs, completed route, and reported internal setup WNS **+2.280 ns** and hold WHS **+0.157 ns** against the 8 ns clock. The standalone I/O ports have no input/output delay constraints, so these are internal-path figures, not integration timing. `evidence/timing/c64_seed7_pds_reports.zip` contains device-map, route, and independent timing reports (SHA256 `9FA3D590F5FB983CAC75A576A4106E45DBCD546BF8BD47D4B573E311DEAC0923`). `run_route.tcl` and `strip_reorder.fdc` reproduce the build; the isolated working tree is `D:/FPGA PDS/yx/C64_strip_route_20261028/`.

The C59 trace model predicts 42,024 cache misses at 15 degrees and 82,171 at 90 degrees for 16-line strips with 256 tags, versus 475,750 and 1,176,481 for raster traversal. These are idealized LRU counts, not measured C64 DDR transactions or frame rates. Integration requires changing the coordinate generator to strip-column order, carrying output coordinates through the mapper and cache, checking that simultaneous fill/drain and source fetches fit DDR bandwidth, then running full-frame correctness, complete route, setup/hold timing, and board validation. The prototype does not satisfy the arbitrary-angle real-time target on its own.
